---
description: Start a parallel handover session (a, b or c) from docs/handover
---

Start handover session `$ARGUMENTS`.

1. Confirm your working directory is a dedicated worktree (`git worktree list`),
   not the coordinator checkout, and that you will only use absolute paths under it.
2. Read `docs/handover/preamble.md`, then `docs/handover/session-$ARGUMENTS.md`
   (lower-case the argument). Together they are your task.
3. Then read `CLAUDE.md`, `DECISIONS.md` and `BACKLOG.md`.
4. Restate the task, the files you expect to touch and your reserved decision
   number in three lines, then begin.
